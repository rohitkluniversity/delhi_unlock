Overview

Delhi → Agra → Delhi in a day

Travel on the expressway by private car with your own guide and driver, arrive in Agra before the day-trippers, and stand in front of the Taj Mahal — the most beautiful building in the world. In the afternoon we visit Agra Fort and, if time allows, the riverside Mehtab Bagh gardens for a different view of the Taj.

Frequently asked

Is the Taj closed on any day?

The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays. Plan your trip accordingly.

How long is the drive?

About 3–4 hours each way by car on the Yamuna Expressway.

Can we take the train instead?

Yes — ask us about the Gatimaan Express option, which is faster.
