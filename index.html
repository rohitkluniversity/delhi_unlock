The people behind the walks

Delhi is our home. Let us introduce you.

Delhi Unlocked is being built around a simple idea: the best way to understand a city is to spend time with people who know it. Our local-led experiences connect visitors with Delhi's history, food, culture and everyday life.

Meet Badal — Founder & Local Guide
Badal has spent years guiding visitors through Delhi and loves helping international travellers understand the city beyond the usual monuments. Delhi Unlocked grew from that simple idea: make every visitor feel welcomed while sharing the history, food, culture and everyday stories that make Delhi special.

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Send it with your enquiry and our team will get back to you.

How do I request a tour?

Use the enquiry form below to choose your tour, date and group size. We aim to contact you within 24 hours. A request is not a confirmed booking until we confirm availability with you.

Can I enquire for 1 to 17 people?

Yes, the form accepts group sizes from 1 to 17. Actual availability and suitable arrangements depend on the experience and date.

Are food, transport and monument tickets included?

Inclusions vary by tour. Please confirm the final inclusions, exclusions and any entry fees with us before your booking is confirmed. The displayed price should not be treated as confirmation that every expense is included.

Where do tours start?

Several Delhi walking experiences use Rajiv Chowk Metro Station, Gate 8 as a meeting point. The exact meeting point for your selected experience will be confirmed with you before the tour.

Can I pay in advance?

We will explain the payment method and timing when confirming your tour. Please do not send money to an unverified account.

Is photography allowed on the Sanjay Colony community tour?

Photography is not allowed on this experience. Guests are expected to respect residents' privacy and follow the guide's instructions.

What if the website form does not work?

Email delhiunlock007@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 92204 09077 if the form does not work.
