Overview

A respectful look at everyday Delhi

Sanjay Colony is a working-class neighbourhood in South Delhi shaped by migrants from across India. This is not a “slum tour” — it is a respectful, community-led walk in which residents share their own stories, homes, workshops and hopes. Our guiding principle is dignity: no photographs of residents, no drop-in visits, no pity.

Photography is not permitted on this tour.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Why is photography not allowed?

Because the tour enters a residential area where people live and work. Our promise to the community is that no photographs of residents will be taken.

Is this a “slum tour”?

No. It is a community-led walk focused on dignity, learning and exchange — not voyeurism.

How is the community compensated?

A meaningful portion of the tour price goes directly to the community and to the resident guides who host you.
