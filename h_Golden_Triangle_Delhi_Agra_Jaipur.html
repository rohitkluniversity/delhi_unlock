Overview

India’s classic circuit

Delhi, Agra and Jaipur — the three cities that between them tell the story of northern India. Over three days we travel by private car with your own guide, staying in comfortable hotels and visiting the highlights of each city at a relaxed, human pace.

Itinerary

Day by day

Day 1 · Delhi

  • Qutub Minar, Humayun’s Tomb, India Gate
  • Drive through Lutyens’ New Delhi
  • Evening at leisure, overnight in Delhi

Day 2 · Delhi → Agra

  • Morning drive to Agra via Yamuna Expressway
  • Visit the Taj Mahal in the afternoon light
  • Agra Fort and (optionally) Mehtab Bagh at sunset
  • Overnight in Agra

Day 3 · Agra → Jaipur

  • Drive to Jaipur, stopping at Fatehpur Sikri and Abhaneri stepwell
  • Evening arrival in Jaipur, drop at your hotel or the airport
  • Optional extension: full day in Jaipur (Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal)
