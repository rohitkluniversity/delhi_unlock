Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Send it with your enquiry and our team will get back to you.

How do I request a tour?

Use the enquiry form below to choose your tour, date and group size. We aim to contact you within 24 hours. A request is not a confirmed booking until we confirm availability with you.

Can I enquire for 1 to 17 people?

Yes, the form accepts group sizes from 1 to 17. Actual availability and suitable arrangements depend on the experience and date.

Are food, transport and monument tickets included?

Inclusions vary by tour. Please confirm the final inclusions, exclusions and any entry fees with us before your booking is confirmed. The displayed price should not be treated as confirmation that every expense is included.

Where do tours start?

Several Delhi walking experiences use Rajiv Chowk Metro Station, Gate 8 as a meeting point. The exact meeting point for your selected experience will be confirmed with you before the tour.

Can I pay in advance?

We will explain the payment method and timing when confirming your tour. Please do not send money to an unverified account.

Is photography allowed on the Sanjay Colony community tour?

Photography is not allowed on this experience. Guests are expected to respect residents' privacy and follow the guide's instructions.

What if the website form does not work?

Email delhiunlock007@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 92204 09077 if the form does not work.

Terms & Cancellation Policy

Local experiences. Real Delhi.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand that travel plans can change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be simple and fair.

Tour Cancellation by Guest

  • If you need to cancel your tour, please contact us as soon as possible through WhatsApp or email.
  • Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the scheduled tour are eligible for a full refund if any advance payment has been made.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the tour may be subject to a 20% cancellation fee, with the remaining amount refunded.
  • If a guest does not arrive for the tour without informing us (no-show), a 20% cancellation fee may apply.

Cancellation by Delhi Unlocked

If we need to cancel a tour because of circumstances on our side, we will offer the guest either:

  • A suitable alternative date, or
  • A full refund of any advance payment made for that tour.

Weather & Unforeseen Circumstances

If a tour cannot safely operate because of severe weather, government restrictions, transportation problems, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control, we will work with the guest to reschedule the tour or provide an appropriate refund where applicable.

Booking Policy

  • Tour availability is subject to confirmation.
  • Guests should provide accurate contact information when making an enquiry or booking.
  • Please arrive at the agreed meeting point on time.
  • The tour duration may be affected if a guest arrives late.
  • Some tours involve walking, stairs, crowded areas, public transport and uneven roads. Guests should choose a tour appropriate for their physical ability.

Payment Policy

Payment arrangements will be clearly communicated before the tour.

If an advance payment is required, the amount and payment method will be confirmed with the guest before payment is made.

For tours where payment is made after the tour, the guest should pay the agreed amount directly to the guide at the end of the tour.

Photography & Guest Privacy

We want every guest to feel comfortable and safe during their experience with Delhi Unlocked.

Our guides may sometimes take photographs during a tour to help guests remember their experience. We will not use a guest's photograph for advertising, social media, our website, promotional materials, or any other public purpose without the guest's permission.

If a photograph includes you and you do not want it to be used publicly, please let your guide or our team know. Your privacy and comfort come first.

Complaints, Feedback & Guest Comfort

Your comfort and safety are important to us.

If you have a complaint, concern or any problem during your tour, please speak to your guide or contact the Delhi Unlocked team as soon as possible. We will listen to your concern and do our best to help resolve the issue quickly.

If at any time you feel uncomfortable, unsafe or unhappy with any part of the experience, please tell your guide or our team immediately. We take such concerns seriously and will do our best to support you.

We also welcome feedback after your tour because it helps us improve our experiences and provide better service to future guests.

Privacy Policy

Delhi Unlocked respects your privacy.

When you contact us or make a booking enquiry, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, tour requirements and other information necessary to arrange your tour.

We use this information only for purposes such as:

  • Responding to your enquiry.
  • Arranging and managing your tour.
  • Communicating with you about your booking.
  • Providing customer support.

We do not intentionally sell your personal information to third parties.

We may use third-party services such as WhatsApp, email, website hosting, payment providers or booking platforms to communicate with you or process your booking. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

Terms & Conditions

By booking or participating in a Delhi Unlocked tour, you agree to provide accurate information and follow reasonable instructions from your guide.

Guests are responsible for their personal belongings during the tour. Delhi Unlocked is not responsible for loss, theft or damage to personal belongings except where required by applicable law.

Guests are responsible for informing us in advance about any important mobility requirements, dietary requirements or other circumstances that may affect the tour.

We reserve the right to refuse or end a tour where a guest behaves in an abusive, threatening, dangerous or seriously disruptive manner.

Tour itineraries may occasionally change because of traffic, closures, weather, security restrictions, public events or other circumstances outside our control.

Contact

For cancellations, refunds, booking questions, complaints, feedback or any other concerns, please contact Delhi Unlocked through the contact details provided on our website.
