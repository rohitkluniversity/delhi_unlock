Overview

Travel like a Delhiite

Delhi’s metro is fast, clean, cheap and one of the best ways to understand how this giant city hangs together. On this tour we ride the network between the two great halves of Delhi — the crowded bazaars of Shahjahanabad (Old Delhi) and the wide, green avenues of Lutyens’ New Delhi — with a local guide who uses the metro every day.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Is the metro crowded?

Sometimes yes — that is part of the experience. We travel outside the very busiest rush hours and your guide manages the group.

Do I need a metro card?

No — fares are covered by us and your guide carries the tokens/cards.

Is this suitable for children?

Yes, children generally enjoy the ride and the variety of stops.
