Old Delhi Food Tour

Taste Old Delhi’s iconic street food and discover the stories behind each bite.

About this experience

Old Delhi is one of the best places in India to eat. This local-led food tour introduces you to classic street foods, sweet shops, spice markets and family recipes. Your guide will help you navigate the lanes, choose safe and delicious stops, and explain the cultural history behind the food.

Details

Duration: 3–4 hours

Meeting point: Rajiv Chowk Metro Station, Gate 8

Price: Ask
