Overview

Eat like a Delhiite

Old Delhi is India’s greatest street-food city, and this walk is the tastiest way to understand it. We stop at family-run stalls that have been perfecting their recipes for generations — parathas fried in ghee, spiced chaat, seekh kebabs, jalebi and slow-cooked kulfi — with plenty of chai to wash it all down.

On the menu

What you will taste

  • Parathas from the legendary Paranthe Wali Gali
  • Street chaat with tamarind and yoghurt
  • Seekh kebabs from a 100-year-old grill
  • Buttery daulat ki chaat (seasonal)
  • Jalebi, imarti and hot jalebi with rabri
  • Kulfi and falooda
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian routes available
  • Hygiene-first stall selection
  • Plenty of chai stops
  • Small groups
  • Option to skip any dish

Good to know

Frequently asked

I’m vegetarian — can I join?

Absolutely. Vegetarian and mixed routes are both available; tell us your preference when you book.

How spicy is the food?

Your guide will ask the stalls to adjust spice to your comfort level.

Is water safe to drink?

We provide bottled water throughout and stick to stalls known for safe preparation.

Can I bring children?

Yes — children are welcome and kids usually love this tour.
