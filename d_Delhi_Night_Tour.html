Overview

The capital after dark

When the traffic thins and the monuments light up, Delhi becomes a different city. This evening tour takes in the great ceremonial vistas of New Delhi — India Gate, the wide sweep of Kartavya Path — plus a beautifully lit gurdwara and the bustle of Connaught Place after dark.

What to expect

Highlights of the evening

  • Rashtrapati Bhavan and the Government District by night
  • Gurdwara Bangla Sahib after dark
  • Connaught Place’s inner circle
  • Optional chai and dessert stop

Good to know

Frequently asked

Is it safe at night?

Yes — you are accompanied by a local guide and private driver throughout.

Will we go inside monuments?

Most monuments are closed at night; we view them beautifully illuminated from outside and visit the gurdwara, which is open.

Can I be dropped at my hotel?

Yes, hotel drop-off within central Delhi is included on request.
